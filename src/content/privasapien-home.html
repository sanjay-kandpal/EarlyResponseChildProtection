Continuous Sensing

Watch, hubs and personal-baseline calibration

The Watch — heart rate & ECG (MAX86150), EDA skin-sweat, and motion sensing in an ordinary-looking timepiece.

Home Hubs — mains-powered room listeners that detect raised voices, crying and impact sounds on-device.

Personal baseline — each child's readings are measured against their own calibrated normal, not a generic average.

45-second polling with instant ramp-up — the watch switches to high-frequency sensing the moment a trigger fires.

EDA catches the freeze response — a frightened child who goes very still, whom heart rate alone might miss.

SafeWatch

SafeWatch is a four-layer early-warning and response system that embeds privacy, safety, and human judgment directly into how a child's household is monitored. From continuous sensing to Protection Officer oversight, it delivers built-in controls across the entire response lifecycle — enabling MSF's Protective Service to detect distress early, intervene in time, and preserve families with confidence.

Privacy as a Profit Center: Unlock Data, AI, Agents & Trust

Turning continuous, privacy-preserving signals into earlier interventions, fewer escalations, and safer family-preservation outcomes

Continuous Sensing

Continuous, privacy-preserving sensing fuses a child-worn watch — heart rate, ECG, EDA and motion — with mains-powered home hubs that detect household acoustic distress: raised voices, crying, impact sounds.

Consent

Monitoring is a safety-plan condition under CYPA authority, agreed by the non-offending caregiver.

Documented alongside the existing safety plan and proportionate to the assessed risk of the case.

Age-appropriate assent explains the watch to children in child-friendly terms, respecting their dignity.

Data is non-evidentiary — used only to trigger safety responses, never in legal proceedings.

On-Device Privacy in Action

Only Event Labels Leave the Home — Never Audio

Privacy by Structure

On-device speech filtering strips words before any other processing happens.

Only confidence-scored event labels are transmitted to the cloud.

Physiological readings travel over encrypted BLE — raw waveforms never leave the watch.

No camera or video is used anywhere; the cloud stores metadata, not biometric signals.

Structural guarantees mean a breach cannot reconstruct speech or identify a child.

Detection & Fusion — How the ACI Decides

The Arousal Correlation Index fires only when both a child's physiological Z-score and household acoustic confidence exceed threshold within the same short window — a rule-based, interpretable, auditable decision, not a black box.

Detection Pipeline

SafeWatch runs two independent models — a Z-score physiological detector and an acoustic classifier — combined by a rule-based fusion engine that requires both signals before it ever raises an alert.

Signal Fusion

Continuous evaluation of each child's signals against their own baseline, with real-time ACI risk scoring.

Detection & Fusion

Personal baseline, dual-signal fusion, and transparent thresholds

2) Dual-signal fusion — physiological + acoustic

3) Arousal Correlation Index (ACI) risk scoring, 0–100

4) Rule-based, interpretable thresholds tuned from labelled data

Early Warning & Response

The Tiered Response Ladder

1) Tier 1 — neutral, non-accusatory nudge to the at-risk adult

2) Tier 2 — alert to the designated safe adult (WhatsApp/SMS)

3) Monitoring loop adapts to the safe adult's stated ETA

4) Tier 3 — escalation to the assigned Protection Officer

5) The PO — not the system — makes every further decision

Who It's For

SafeWatch sits where child protection meets everyday family life — built for the people accountable for a child's safe return home.

Protection Officer

Continuous household safety assurance between visits — less routine monitoring burden, more time for the families who need you most.

Safe Adult

A timely, non-alarming alert when a child may be in distress — so you can step in early, before a situation escalates.

Caregiver

Reassurance that your child is watched over privately and respectfully — no cameras, no recorded conversations in your home.

MSF / Protective Service

Evidence-based, privacy-preserving monitoring that supports family-preservation decisions and reduces recurrence of harm.

Data Protection Officer

Child Psychologist
